About Me

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Old Dominion University, with a joint appointment in the School of Cybersecurity. I joined ODU in July 2025 as part of its Trustworthy AI cluster hire.

My research focuses on the security, robustness, and trustworthy applications of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), with emphasis on adversarial robustness, prompt optimization, secure code generation, hardware design automation, Internet and IoT security, and deepfake detection.

Research Interests

  • Adversarial robustness of GNNs and LLMs in cybersecurity, software security, and trustworthy AI applications
  • Prompt optimization and engineering
  • Secure and functional source code generation
  • Hardware design automation with LLMs and GNNs
  • Internet and IoT security
  • Deepfake detection and multimodal deepfake detection
  • Hallucination detection, agentic AI security, and agent deputy attacks
